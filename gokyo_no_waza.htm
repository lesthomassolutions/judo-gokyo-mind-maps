Instructions for the
Gokyo No Waza, Happo No Kuzushi, Ranraku Nagewaza Mind Map Builder
The starting point for this tool is the Gokyo No Waza page.  Each of the 40 throws are layed out in the area of Uke's direction when thrown.  

 - Note the position of Uke's feet (#1).  

 - Each throw is color coded with the associated Gokyo No Waza group.  Clicking a group will filter for the techniques of that group  (#2).  

 - Additional filtering for Tachi Waza and Sutemi Waza techniques available by clicking those buttons (#3).
Clicking on a technique will display the associated page idenfied by its name (#4).  
Also displayed:

 - English translation of the technique (#5).

 - Classification of technique (#6).

 - Images depicting the technique (#7).  

 - Direction of applying technique on Uke is indicated with a green Success button (#8).

 - Renraku Waza attacks surround the Happo No Kuzushi diagram.
  
 - Link to Gokyo No Waza start page (#9).  

 - Link to print displayed map (#10).

 - Link to YouTube video (#11).  Note:  If you click the X to close the video panel before the video completes, the audio will continue to play in the background.
