Introduction to the Gokyo No Waza, Happo No Kuzushi, Renraku Nagewaza Mind Map Builder.
(Introduction to the 40 original Judo throws of the Kodokan, Eight Directions of Off Balance, Combination Standing Techniques Mind Map Builder)

A Classic Right Sided Perspective.

This tool is intended to help the judoka student understand the direction of attack for each of the 40 Goko No Waza, and the variations for optional attacks should the attempted attack fail.

It is possible for a judoka to write an entire paper on the intricacies of each step of transition from a failed technique into the attack of another technique.  This tool is a big picture application, the micro details necessary to successfully perform a transition from one throw to another takes much study and practice and is not learned through the use of this online tool alone.

May you use this tool in good health and understanding.
